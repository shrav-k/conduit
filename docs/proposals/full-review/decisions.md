Some fix defects, ambiguities and contradictions whose fix adds no behavior (rulings 37 and 46 point 4). Others settle small findings (ruling 71 point 4). Each is logged for you to strike (ruling 48), and a struck fix is withdrawn with its case. Until the S1 rerun, no small feature that could move S1's author counts is made: no CLI flag, short-form key, template or default (`IN_FORCE.md:307`).

How to read the tables. "Unsafe" marks a fix that closes a way in which an engine keeping every promise could still do harm. Those come first in each table. "Vector" marks a fix that comes with a conformance case. "Small feature" marks a fix made under ruling 71 point 4. "Review list" means the fix also goes on the list for our joint review. `when`, `then` and `observable` are a promise's situation, obligation and check (`spec/README.md:290-292`). Where a fix depends on one of your questions, the row says so.
