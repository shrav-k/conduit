# The owner's direction for Projects, 2026-11-10

Not binding. The [specification](../../../spec/README.md) alone binds. The owner's direction for
the Project concept, given in discussion after the [language-shape proposal](../language-shape/README.md),
recorded as the basis for the investigation that follows. It is direction, not a ruling: the
rulings it needs are put to the owner once the investigation reports.

## How it came about

The proposal had a Project home every object, Pools shared by lists declared on the Pool, two-sided
authority and approvals by people. The owner found faults with it: "I don't think pools themselves
should declare who can use them, the interface should still be able to give conduits to projects to
manage them directly. Doesn't the root interface have discovery and admin power over everything?
Projects should be able to still open admin conduits to pools." The coordinator agreed: a list on a
Pool is a grant object in disguise, which rulings 27 to 29 exclude, since opening a Conduit already
needs authority over its target. The owner then asked: "What if agents were tied to projects like
resources are tied to pools?", and on the combined model: "This matches my intention! … I like the
idea of root having conduits to all projects and pools by default also."

## The direction

- **A Project is to its Agents what a Pool is to its Resources.** Each Agent is owned by exactly one
  Project for its life. Administering an Agent (registering, setting it up, retiring, inspecting) is
  authorized through a Conduit to its Project, as a Resource's is through its Pool. Retiring a
  Project with its Agents retires them. Owning permits nothing: using an Agent, such as running its
  turns, is a Conduit to the Agent.
- **Pools are independent.** No Project owns a Pool. A Project refers to Pools by holding Conduits to
  them.
- **A Project can hold Conduits**: to Pools, to administer them, and to `access` within a bound, to
  grant its own Agents use of Resources. Actors given authority over a Project act through the
  Conduits it holds.
- **Interfaces have authority over one or more Projects**, by Conduits to them; that is how
  Interfaces become granular. Interfaces themselves stay at the engine level.
- **Root keeps discovery and administration over everything**, and holds a Conduit to every Project
  and every Pool by default, made with each and shown.
- **No Pool declares who may use it.** Every permission stays an open Conduit.

## What follows

A thorough investigation of this model, with designs, skeptics and prior art, then the rulings it
needs, put to the owner one by one.
