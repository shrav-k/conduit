# Ruling 57: historical documents may be edited and condensed

Status: **ruled by the owner on 2026-11-10** (plan-audit ruling 57). This is the record of a
ruling. Where it and the specification differ, the specification governs.

The owner asked to condense the repository's historical documents: "we should now figure out
whether we need to start refactoring our experiments and condense our historical documents by
removing what's no longer relevant as it will pollute our context. I'm not concerned with keeping a
perfect historical record as long as all relevant conduit information is preserved." The coordinator
noted that a rule in `AGENTS.md` forbids editing `docs/archive/` and the content of `docs/research/`,
as records of their time. The owner: "I revoke the rule saying you cannot edit those docs".

## The decisions

- **The rule is revoked.** `docs/archive/` and `docs/research/` may be edited, condensed or removed
  like any other document that does not bind.
- **The standard is relevance, not a perfect record.** A document may be removed when every piece of
  relevant Conduit information in it is preserved elsewhere: in `spec/`, or in a live document. Git
  history keeps every removed version, and ruling 39's practice of listing removed files with a
  link to their last version applies.
- The form of ruling records and how duplicated content is removed are being discussed.

## What follows

`AGENTS.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` drop the rule. The condensation audit's plan is fitted to the form
of records the owner settles.
