## Differences from the area drafts

The drafts' optional/no expiry becomes a required expiry for every present kind. Their review
and keep machinery and old PND numbering do not land. The draft's evidence in structuredContent
and user-audience content cannot protect codes or fingerprints under K1, so those values are
panel-only. Its elicitationId, completion notice, invalid capability failure and task retry
model are replaced by K1. The account-check draft's resumed held request and reconciliation
clearing failed undo are replaced by C4. The draft's two-hook declaration requires all three
hooks. Its Resource sign-in through management is replaced by C21. Its broad root-management
sign-in addition is replaced by C8. The unavailable wording for held model work follows the
plan's reading of ruling 49's ordinary word “refused”; failed attempts and access refusals
keep their distinct meanings. Each refusing rule gives its true cause, rather than claiming
no operation is listed where a restriction or reservation is the actual cause.

## Review choices

The schema gives request records optional ruleId, refusalTier and pendingAction fields and a
separate answer member. Codes remain absent from any request record and any wire rendering.
Each consumed retry state is replaced by a fresh protected state while the same invocation
continues; replay of a consumed state is rejected. Already-answered setup IDs retain their
answer across computation and purge. The raised-at check for a further answer uses retained
subjects and current scope so authorized repeats conflict instead of disclosing history to
unauthorized callers. A spelling that cannot identify an unknown tool's operation is echoed
without reconstructing hidden names. pendingAction is consistently an array of action IDs.

The conflict list, by path and zero-based step, is in
[vector-impact-038a.md](vector-impact-038a.md). It includes all 31 explicit diagnostic-absence
assertions, 58 bare error-text assertions, and 6 root-management list assertions; categories
overlap. No vector was changed, and passing the old engine suite does not claim it implements
the new promises. Enrollment, provider implementation, new vectors and the management gap
remain with their assigned orders; no work outside this order's scope was performed.
