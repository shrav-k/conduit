## What and why

The full review of 2026-11-14 (order 9 of [its orders](../proposals/full-review/orders.md)) found
that no model has run on the text that orders 077, 081 and 082 wrote: the ruled reading of the
fence was modelled, order 077's wording was not
([FR-447](../proposals/full-review/findings/G1a-model-checking-register.md#fr-447) point 3;
[FR-442](../proposals/full-review/findings/E05-falsifier.md#fr-442) as its verifier corrected it).
It asked for MC2, the operation model of
[experiment 012](../../experiments/012-model-checking/README.md), to run again on 077's text with
its expected refutations registered first
([FR-446](../proposals/full-review/findings/G1a-model-checking-register.md#fr-446) row 1, as its
verifier corrected it). It expected the fence's property E refuted
([FR-180](../proposals/full-review/findings/X03-model-checking.md#fr-180), with
[FR-346](../proposals/full-review/findings/P07-engine-build.md#fr-346)'s escaped child as one
route), and W refuted in the deduplicating variant
([FR-181](../proposals/full-review/findings/X03-model-checking.md#fr-181)).

[Ruling 78](../proposals/ruling-78/README.md#the-decisions), point 6, then dropped the fence and
the record "did not occur": an unknown outcome stays unknown until it is recorded as having
occurred, and, by F13, where a later attempt is answered failed while an earlier attempt of the
same invocation has no answer, the invocation stays unknown. Both are spec pending: `spec/` at
`632881df` still holds the fence, and OPS-012 lacks F13 (IN_FORCE.md, topic 7). So this order cuts
what tested the fence and checks what is left. MC2 reads ruling 78's rule; the withdrawn record
becomes a mutant that must refute O again (MC2-001); and OPS-012 as written is expected to refute
W. The result goes to SO-B, which writes F13 into OPS-012, and to the order that writes ruling 78
point 6 into `spec/`. It is recorded in the account that order 153 made (D28, D31, D33 and D35 of
[the decisions](../proposals/full-review/decisions.md)).

## The decisions it applies

Places are in `experiments/012-model-checking/` on `main` `632881df`.

1. **No fence is modelled.** Property E, the fence's events and their witnesses, and the mutants
   FenceOnTimer, FenceByOtherAttempt and FenceOnSignal are not built. The variant `Fenced` and its
   guard that reads the queues go (`src/operations.rs:270-275`, `:521-528`, `:677-684`). No mutant
   reconciles a settled invocation, as
   [FR-187](../proposals/full-review/findings/X03-model-checking.md#fr-187) asked: a reconciliation
   that can only record that the effect occurred cannot dispatch an invocation again or record a
   false outcome, so it would have nothing to refute.
2. **The correct models read ruling 78 point 6**, in `src/operations.rs`:
   - A reconciliation records only that the effect occurred. `Reconcile` is enabled for an
     invocation whose outcome is unknown and whose effect is present, and records succeeded
     (`:521-528`, `:568-577`).
   - The implementation may answer failed, having applied nothing (OPS-003, IFC-009), to a
     dispatch of an invocation whose effect it has not applied; otherwise it applies and answers
     succeeded, as now (`:422-442`). It gives at most one failed answer in a run, and the search,
     not the script, chooses where. A delivery's step is fixed, so the choice is an action of the
     implementation's own, for example one that arms its next dispatch to be answered failed.
     `model` states this bound in its configuration's `bounds` text, as `revisions::model` sets
     its own (`src/revisions.rs:74-77`); `mc2::configuration` does not change. A plain server's
     error, which IFC-010 makes unknown, is not modelled apart: it leaves the outcome unknown, as a
     lost answer does.
   - F13, in every variant but `DedupFailedSettles`: a failed answer to the invocation's current
     attempt, while an earlier attempt has no answer at the engine, leaves the outcome unknown;
     otherwise it records failed. The engine, except in `DiscardLate`, notes every answer that
     reaches it, a late one too, and keeps them with its index; a late answer still changes no
     outcome (OPS-013, `:443-454`).
   - The correct variants are `Plain`, `Deduplicating` and a new `DiscardLate`: `Deduplicating`
     under DL2's other reading, an engine that discards a late answer, so that an attempt answered
     only late counts as unanswered under F13 (FR-446's DL2 variant, which now bears on F13 rather
     than on a fence). It is expected to give `Deduplicating`'s verdicts: an attempt whose answer
     reached the engine, late or not, has been applied or has had the one failed answer, so it
     cannot land again, and counting it unanswered only keeps an outcome unknown. The run shows
     that DL2 does not bear on W. Every deduplicating variant has the ledger's deduplication fact
     and the deduplicating branches (`:307`, `:374-377`, `:428`).
3. **W also covers an effect that lands later.** W (`RIGHT`, `:588-605`) keeps its two checks at
   the record. It also fails where an effect is applied while the invocation stands failed: after a
   failed record and before any later dispatch of the invocation, as `Ledger::current`
   (`:163-169`) reads its history (FR-446 row 1: "is or becomes present"). An effect of a new
   attempt that OPS-024 dispatches after failed follows that dispatch, so it does not count. Its
   name and promises stay.
4. **The mutants.** Each is one change from a correct variant:

   - `BlindReplay`, `VolatileIndex`, `TimerFailed`, `LateReply`, `KeyOnly` and `RepeatSucceeded`,
     from the new `Plain`, each with its target (`:44-54`).
5. **Parts.** The seven parts and the scope configuration stay as `src/mc2.rs` gives them. Every
