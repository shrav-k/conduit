## Second reading, for two subscriptions with each vendor

Work order 190 added this reading on 2026-11-16, before any page was read, for A5's case,
as [FR-435](../../docs/proposals/full-review/findings/E04-signin-isolation.md#fr-435) asks.
One person holds a primary and a second subscription with each vendor, used through unmodified
Claude Code and Codex Agents in separate homes, with several running at once and for days.

1. **More than one subscription for one person.** Does any page allow, limit or forbid one person holding more than one account, or more than one paid subscription of the same kind, with the vendor? Does any page say that an account is for one person?
2. **A second subscription and the usage limits.** Does any page say whether a second subscription may add to one person's usage, or forbid getting round a usage limit or a rate limit, through more than one account or otherwise? Quote the sentence that comes closest, and say so if none does.
3. **Several Agents, at once and for days.** What do the pages say of a subscription used through the vendor's own client by automated, scripted or programmatic means, by several sessions at once, or without a break over days? Read for Anthropic's “ordinary, individual usage” and its context, for OpenAI's terms on automated or programmatic use, and for the Codex pages on automation with a ChatGPT sign-in.
4. **Who may use an account.** What do the pages say of sharing an account or its credentials, of another person or of software using it, and of the holder's own automation acting on it?
5. **One account in several homes or machines.** What do the pages say of one account signed in on more than one client, home or machine at once, each signed in on its own?
6. **What changed since 2026-11-10.** For each quotation in experiment 010's results of 2026-11-10, does the live page still hold it word for word? Answer: the same; changed, with the new words quoted; no longer found; or the page could not be read. Check the two OpenAI phrases that experiment 010 took from a search, “automatically or programmatically extract data or Output” and the bar on sharing account credentials, against OA1 itself, or record them as still unchecked.

The reading decides nothing. For each reading that bears on a ruling, it drafts a question for
the owner.
