## Standings

Standings count each promise once. A model-run standing means only the bounded projection in
its source accounting, with the order's applied reading, not every clause of the promise. A
contested standing records a finding involving that promise even where its individual
predicate holds; the note distinguishes a refuted predicate, a conflicting reading and
supporting context. This avoids counting a local success as resolution of an outstanding
composition finding. The named model is the later order's accounting entry; earlier models
remain in the notes.

The model-run standings require `model`, `read` and `digest`:

- `searched`: the property relates events of different steps, so an order of events or a
  fault that the search explores could refute it.
- `restated`: restated at decision time; the property reads what the decision computed in the
  same step.
- `holds, not graded`: held in its model and not yet graded. It is graded at that model's next
  run, where each mutant also runs with every interleaving fault switched off. A property that
  this single schedule refutes is a one-step check, and its rows become `restated` or `static`.
- `contradiction: F`, `gap: F`, `ambiguity: F` and `property over-broad: F`, as the account of
  2026-11-11 classes them. F is a model finding matching `MC[0-9]+[a-z]?-[0-9]{3}`, such as
  `MC4-009`.

The other standings are:

- `text changed, not rerun`: the row had a model-run standing, and its promise's text has
  changed since `read`. `was` holds the standing it had; `model`, `read` and `digest` are
  required and stay as they were.
- `static`: decided in one step, or held in its model only by a property that nothing the
  search varies can reach (FR-456's mark K). A vector or a contract test shows it, not a model.
  `outside` may list repository paths of evidence outside the conformance cases. The cases are
  counted from the `index.yaml` files under `conformance/`, and never copied into the account.
- `not modelled`: no property checks its safety slice; the note says why.
- `left to MC9`: assigned to a model not yet written.

`model` is optional on the last three standings. It is written as `module::key`, with any
further keys after commas (for example `gates::H, C, R`). `module` names a file
`experiments/012-model-checking/src/<module>.rs`.
